# Support Channel Guide

The support channel is home for any of your questions and any issues you encounter during your stay with us, don't hesitate to you use it as much as you need!

## How do I use it?
First, open the `support🫡` channel from the left side panel of the server. Click on the `New Post` button. 

![create post ](https://i.postimg.cc/pL6PZ4yS/createpost.png)

Write your question in **as much detail as possible**, include pictures if possible as well. 

![](https://i.postimg.cc/rF5xSxjc/question.png)

This post you create will open a mini-chat where you can talk further about your problem with one of our supporters. This chat will be open and available for you until your issue is solved.

![chat](https://i.postimg.cc/X7Wp6hfQ/post.png)


## Some Notes
- Check previous posts from the tags related to your problem, you might find your solution from someone who encountered a similar issue to yours.
![tags](https://i.postimg.cc/zBm8mhCC/tags.png)
 - Our supporters will be notified and will help you out as soon as possible!
